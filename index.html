Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Shivam Mobile Shop (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and how we keep it safe.

1. Information we collect

  • Your name, mobile number, address and email (if you share it).
  • Documents needed for your work, such as Aadhaar, PAN, photographs and bank details.
  • Passenger name, age, ID and travel details for ticket bookings.
  • Basic sender and receiver details for money transfers.

2. How we use it

We use your information only to complete the work you ask for (such as Aadhaar, PAN, ticket booking or money transfer), to contact you about it, and to meet legal requirements.

3. Keeping your documents safe

Your documents are kept only until your work is complete. Afterwards we make every effort to delete scanned copies and photos from our devices, unless the law requires us to keep them.

4. Sharing

We never sell your information. It is shared only with government portals, banks, and payment or booking partners, and only as much as the work requires. We may also disclose it if required by law.

5. OTPs and passwords

Any OTP, PIN or password is used only during your work, in your presence. We do not save it or share it with anyone.

6. Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask for corrections, or request deletion by contacting us at the shop.

7. Website and cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or advertising.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
